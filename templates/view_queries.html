View-Based Queries

Execute analytical queries on database views (QV1-QV4)

QV1: Hospitals in Cityville

Retrieve hospitals that have a location in 'Cityville'

SELECT * FROM HospitalLocationSummary WHERE Locations LIKE '%Cityville%';

QV2: Patients Over 30 Years

Retrieve names of patients who are over 30 years old

SELECT PatientName FROM PatientAgeDistribution WHERE Age > 30;

QV3: Physician with Most Specialties

Find the physician with the highest number of specialties

SELECT FullName FROM PhysicianSpecialityCount ORDER BY NumSpecialities DESC LIMIT 1;

QV4: Average Patient Age

Calculate the average age of all patients

SELECT AVG(Age) AS AverageAge FROM PatientAgeDistribution;
